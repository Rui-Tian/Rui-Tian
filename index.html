Publications

[1] A potent neutralizing and protective antibody against a conserved continuous epitope on HSV glycoprotein D [PDF] Rui Tian, et al. Antiviral Research, 2022.
Main Content: This study identified a potent monoclonal antibody, 4A3, which targets a highly conserved continuous epitope on glycoprotein D to provide broad protection against both HSV-1 and HSV-2 by blocking viral entry and cell-to-cell spread.
Contribution: Manuscript writing, data processing, antibody identification & evaluation, cell culture, and epitope exploration.
[2] Oncolytic virus expressing PD-1 inhibitors activates collaborative intratumoral immune response [PDF] F. Ju, ..., Rui Tian, et al. Journal for ImmunoTherapy of Cancer, 2022.
Main Content: This study demonstrates that an engineered oncolytic herpes simplex virus expressing humanized PD-1 inhibitors (YST-OVH) effectively remodels the tumor microenvironment to promote systemic antitumor immunity and synergizes with CTLA-4 or TIM-3 blockade for enhanced therapeutic efficacy.
Contribution: Involved in cell culture, viral production, titration, and data analysis.
[3] The tumor suppressor DACT3 sensitizes TNBC to apatinib via Wnt/β-catenin pathway [PDF] J. Wu, Rui Tian, et al. Translational Oncology, 2025.
Main Content: This study demonstrates that the tumor suppressor DACT3 sensitizes triple-negative breast cancer to apatinib by inhibiting the Wnt/β-catenin signaling pathway, providing a potential biomarker and therapeutic strategy to overcome drug resistance.
Contribution: Molecular/cellular guidance, data analysis, and manuscript revision.
[4] The novel DNA methylation marker FIBIN suppresses non-small cell lung cancer metastasis [PDF] M. Peng, ..., Rui Tian, et al. Cellular Signalling, 2024.
Main Content: This study identifies FIBIN as a novel DNA methylation marker that is frequently silenced by promoter hypermethylation in non-small cell lung cancer, acting as a tumor suppressor to inhibit proliferation and metastasis by negatively regulating ANXA2 and inactivating the Wnt/β-catenin signaling pathway.
Contribution: Involved in data analysis and manuscript revision.
[5] PDCD2 as a prognostic biomarker in glioma correlates with malignant phenotype [PDF] F. Dai, ..., Rui Tian, et al. Genes & Diseases, 2023.
Main Content: This study demonstrates that PDCD2 is significantly overexpressed in glioma and serves as a prognostic biomarker, where its knockdown effectively inhibits tumor cell proliferation, migration, and invasion.
Contribution: Involved in data analysis and manuscript revision.
